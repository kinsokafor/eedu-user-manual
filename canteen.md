# Canteen

Portal: `/eedu/canteen/`

Canteen (tuck-shop) staff sell food and drinks to students. Students pay from their **wallet** by showing their ID card, so no cash changes hands at the counter. Every sale is recorded against the student and posted to the school's accounts.

Read [Getting started](getting-started.md) first. A phone or tablet with a camera works best at the counter.

## Making a sale

**Canteen → New Sale**

1. **Find the student:**
   - **Scan ID card:** hold the card to the camera. It closes by itself when it reads a valid card from this school.
   - Or search by name or passcode and choose **Find**.
2. The student's card shows their **photo** (check it's them), their **class**, their **wallet balance**, and what they have **spent today**.
3. Tap the items they are buying. Tap again to add more, or use **−** and **+** to change quantities.
4. Check the **Total**:
   - If it's more than the balance, it shows in red: "More than the ₦… available". Remove items.
   - If the school has a **daily spending limit**, a sale that goes over it is refused.
5. Choose **Charge ₦…**. A green message shows the amount charged and the student's **new balance**, and the screen is ready for the next student straight away.
6. To pick a different student before charging, choose **Change**. **Done** takes you back to the dashboard.

Rules:
- A student can never spend more than their balance, and two sales can't spend the same money at once.
- If a sale was made in error, you can't undo it yourself. Ask the bursar to **void** it: the money goes back to the student's wallet.

### When a student has no money
Wallets are topped up at the **bursary** (cash or bank transfer), and money a parent pays online beyond the fees due also goes into the wallet. Send the student to the bursary. You can't take cash top-ups at the canteen.

## Items and prices

**Canteen → Items & Prices**
- **Add item:** a **Name**, a **Price** and a **Status** (*Active* items appear on the sale screen).
- **Edit** an item to change its name or price. A new price applies to new sales only.
- An item that has been sold can't be deleted. Set it to **Inactive** to hide it.

---

## End of day

The bursar and School Admin see the full **Canteen Sales** report (by date, by item, and each sale with who sold it). Your dashboard shows today's totals, so compare them with your own records before you close.
