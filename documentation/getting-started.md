# Getting Started and Everyday Tasks

This guide is for everyone who uses eedu.ng: staff, students and parents. Your role's own manual builds on it.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open your school's eedu.ng address and choose **Login** (or go straight to `/home/#/login`).
2. In **Email or Passcode**, enter your email address or your passcode. Your passcode is printed on your ID card.
3. Enter your password and choose **Login**.

**Your first password is your passcode.** Every new account, staff or student, starts with its passcode as its password.

- **Staff** still using that first password are taken to **Change Password** when they sign in, once a day, until they change it. Please change it the first time.
- **Students** are not forced to change it, but should, especially on a shared computer.

After signing in you land on your role's portal:

| Role | Portal address |
|---|---|
| Software Engineer, Portal Manager | `/eedu/admin/` |
| School Admin | `/eedu/school-admin/` |
| Dean | `/eedu/dean/` |
| HOD | `/eedu/hod/` |
| Teacher | `/eedu/teacher/` |
| Form Teacher | `/eedu/form-teacher/` |
| Bursar | `/eedu/bursar/` |
| Security | `/eedu/security/` |
| Canteen | `/eedu/canteen/` |
| Student | `/eedu/student/` |

### Forgotten password

- **If your account has an email address:** on the Login page choose **forgot password?**, enter your email and follow the link sent to you.
- **If it doesn't (most students):** ask a School Admin or Portal Manager. They open your profile and choose **Change password** to set a new one.

## 2. Finding your way around

### The sidebar

- **Menus for your role** are grouped under headings such as *My Teaching*, *Academics*, *People*, *Money*, *Gate*, *Communicate* and *School*. An item with an arrow opens a sub-menu, and the page you're on is highlighted.
- **Half-collapsed menu (computers and tablets).** The arrow button at the bottom of the sidebar shrinks it to a narrow strip of icons, leaving more room for the page. Hover over or click an icon to see its name; an icon with a sub-menu opens it as a small card beside the strip. Click the arrow again to bring the full sidebar back.
- **On a phone** the sidebar opens over the page from the menu button at the top left, and closes when you choose a page. The Student, Security and Canteen portals also have a bar of their most-used pages along the bottom of the screen; **More** opens the full menu.
- **🔒 locked items:** a menu item your school's plan doesn't include is greyed out with a padlock (shown to Portal Managers, School Admins and Software Engineers), or hidden for everyone else.

### The top bar

From left to right, depending on your role:

- **Term**: the active session and term, so you always know which term you're working in.
- **Search** (🔍): find a student, or a member of staff, by name or passcode, and jump straight to their profile. Teaching, academic and bursary staff can search students; Bursars, School Admins and Portal Managers can search staff too.
- **School** (staff in more than one school): see [Switching schools](#switching-schools).
- **+ New**: a shortcut to what you create most often, for example *Add student*, *Add staff*, *Create exam*, *Record payment*, *Record behaviour*, *New lesson*, *New CBT test* or *Send bulk SMS*. You only see the ones your role and your school's plan allow.
- **Light / dark** (☀ / ☾): see [Light and dark mode](#light-and-dark-mode).
- **Notifications** (🔔), **Messages** (✉) and the **account menu** (your photo): **Profile**, **Change Password**, **Notifications** and **Logout**.

### Switching schools

If you work in more than one school on eedu.ng, the school card at the top of the sidebar says **Switch school**, and the school's logo appears in the top bar. Click either to open the school list:

- **Search** by the school's name, town or ID. Several schools can share a name, so each one shows its address, its current term and its ID.
- **Current** is the school you're in, then **Recent** (the last few you used), then **All schools** from A to Z.
- Use the arrow keys and **Enter**, or click a school. A note at the bottom of the screen confirms *Now working in …*.

The page you're on reloads for the new school. If you were looking at one record (a student, a staff member, an exam or a result), you go to the dashboard instead, because that record belongs to the old school. On a phone the list slides up from the bottom of the screen.

### Light and dark mode

Choose the sun/moon button in the top bar:

- **Light** or **Dark**: always use that look on this device.
- **Same as device** (the default): follow your phone's or computer's own setting, and change with it.

Your choice is remembered on that device only, so a shared computer can look different from your phone.

### Your dashboard

Your start page. At the top, a welcome banner with today's date, your role and the active term, and buttons for your most common jobs. Below it:

- **Needs your attention:** things to act on now, most urgent first, each with a button that takes you there (for example *term dates aren't set* or *55 teaching staff have no subjects assigned*).
- **Headline numbers** for your role (students, staff, fees collected, results entered…), then cards for each part of your work. Each manual describes its role's dashboard.

Cards for Timetable, CBT, Remote Lessons, Bookstore and Pastoral Care only appear when your school's plan includes that feature.

### Locked screens and missing buttons

A screen or button your school's plan doesn't include shows a notice such as *"Bursary (fees and payments) is not included in this school's plan."* It isn't a fault and nothing is lost. See [Plans and subscriptions](plans.md).

## 3. Messages

**Message Center** (in the sidebar) is eedu.ng's internal messaging. At the top of the page, switch between **Inbox** and **Compose**.

- **Inbox:** messages sent to you or to your role, newest first. Unread messages are tinted, with a purple edge. Open one to read the thread and reply.
- **Compose:** choose a **role** in **To** to message everyone with that role at your school (for example, all bursars), write your message and send.
- **Message one person:** open their profile and choose **Message** at the top.

## 4. Things that behave the same everywhere

- **Server clock notice.** Some forms show "The server's clock reads … which is N behind this device". This only means the server keeps time in a different time zone. Times are saved exactly as you enter them.
- **Nothing sensitive is deleted.** Behaviour records, welfare notes, counselling sessions, and canteen and bookstore sales are *withdrawn* or *voided*, with a reason, instead of being deleted. The history stays.
- **Printing.** Screens with a **Print** button (report cards, broadsheets, receipts, class lists, room lists, house tables) open your browser's print window with the school's letterhead at the top. Printing results needs a plan that includes it; otherwise the **Print** button is replaced by a notice.
- **Lists of people.** Student and staff lists can be shown as cards or as a table (the two buttons above the list). In both, a person's name opens their profile.
- **Money** is shown in Naira (₦) unless your school uses another currency.

## 5. Your profile

**Account menu → Profile** shows your details. Software Engineers, Portal Managers, School Admins and Deans can edit other people's details from their profile (**Edit**). Software Engineers, Portal Managers and School Admins can also set a new password for them (**⋯ → Change password**). Everyone can change their own password from **Change Password**.

What a student's or staff member's profile shows to staff (overview, details and records) is described in [School Admin → The student profile](school-admin.md#the-student-profile) and [The staff profile](school-admin.md#the-staff-profile).

## 6. Staying safe

- Never share your password.
- A passcode alone is not a password. Change the first password so that someone who sees your ID card cannot sign in as you.
- Log out on shared computers (**Account menu → Logout**).
- Scratch card PINs and passcode lists are like cash. Keep printed lists locked away.
