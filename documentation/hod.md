# Head of Department (HOD)

Portal: `/eedu/hod/`

The HOD role is for **tertiary institutions**, where classes are *Levels* and streams are *Departments*. An HOD oversees their department's results: creating exams, following score entry across the department, and publishing results. They also teach.

Read [Getting started](getting-started.md) first.

## Your dashboard: department progress

The dashboard shows **today's lessons** (when Timetables are on), the CBT, Remote Lessons and Pastoral Care cards, and a **department progress** panel.

1. Choose an **Exam** at the top.
2. Summary figures show your department's **Subjects**, **Scores entered** and **Not started**.
3. Each subject and level shows:
   - how many students have been scored out of the number enrolled, for example *18 / 42 scored*;
   - the average so far;
   - the other teachers assigned.

**"No department subjects assigned"** means your own staff profile has no subjects. Your department is the set of subjects assigned to you for each level. Ask a School Admin to open your staff profile, choose **Subjects**, and tick your department's subjects for each level.

## Results

You can:
- **create exams** (Examinations → Create Exam);
- enter scores for the subjects assigned to you;
- view broadsheets and publish results, including annual results;
- print results.

Publishing and printing results need a plan that includes them; otherwise the buttons are replaced by a notice. See [Plans and subscriptions](plans.md).

The full step-by-step workflow is in the [Dean manual](dean.md#results):
- creating an exam;
- Update Result, and entering scores from Excel;
- the broadsheet and publishing;
- report cards;
- annual results and promotion;
- tracking the latest score entries.

{% hint style="info" %}
You enter scores only for the subjects assigned to you. Deans, School Admins and Portal Managers can enter any subject.
{% endhint %}

## Timetable

**Academics → My Timetable** shows your lessons for the term once the timetable is published. You can also view any level's published timetable, and print it.

## CBT and Remote Lessons

Use **Academics → E-learning** exactly as a teacher does; see the [Teacher manual](teacher.md#cbt-computer-based-tests). You manage the tests, questions, lessons and assignments for your own subjects.

## Pastoral Care

You can:
- record behaviour;
- see the behaviour entries you recorded;
- see welfare alert labels on any student.

You can see more if you're a form teacher for a level, or have been given a pastoral duty. See the [Pastoral Team manual](pastoral-team.md).

## Students

**Management → School**, open your institution, then **Students**. This lists students by year, semester, level and department, with search. Click a student to open their profile. The profile has **Academic Reports**, **Bio/Meta Data** and, when Pastoral Care is on, the student's pastoral summary.

## Bulk SMS

**Utilities → SMS & Printing → Send Bulk SMS / Bulk SMS Reports.** See the [School Admin manual](school-admin.md#bulk-sms).
