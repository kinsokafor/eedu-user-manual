# Students and Parents

Portal: `/eedu/student/`

Students and parents use the same account: the student's. With it you can:
- see results and print report cards;
- see fees, pay online, and follow the wallet;
- see the timetable;
- read lessons and hand in assignments;
- take CBT tests;
- see the book list and your conduct record.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to the school's eedu.ng address and choose **Login**.
2. In **Email or Passcode**, enter the student's **passcode**. It is on the ID card, for example `E4646561737`.
3. The first password is also the passcode. Please change it afterwards (**Account menu → Change Password**).

Forgot the password? Ask the school office. They can set a new one.

### Quick Portal Access (no password needed)
On the eedu.ng home page, **Quick Portal Access** lets you enter just the passcode and choose **Access Portal**. It shows the student's name and school, then offers:
- **Check Results:** unlock a result with a scratch card PIN (below).
- **Pay Fees:** see what is owed and pay online (below).

## Results

**Academics → Results** lists each published result with the class, the period (term and session), the exam and the average. Open one to see the full report card, and choose **Print Result**.

The report card shows:
- every subject with each part of the marking scheme, the total, grade and remark;
- the overall total, average and position;
- the form teacher's comment;
- the school's notes (for example, next term's date);
- the signatures.

Annual (end-of-session) results appear too, when the school publishes them.

{% hint style="info" %}
A result only appears once the school has **published** it. If your class's results are out but yours isn't, ask the form teacher.
{% endhint %}

### Checking results with a scratch card
Some schools require a **scratch card PIN** to open a result.
1. On the eedu.ng home page, open **Quick Portal Access**, enter the student's passcode, and choose **Check Results**.
2. Enter the **scratch card pin** and choose **Verify**.
3. Choose the result from **Select From Available Results**. It opens.

Please note:
- Each PIN is unique and **unlocks one result for one student**.
- A **used PIN** can still sign you into the student's account, handy if you've forgotten the password, but it **won't unlock a new result**. Buy a new card for each new result.
- "Access is denied. Passcode do not match the owner of the PIN" means the PIN was already used for another student.
- "Another pin has been used for this result" means the result is already unlocked. Sign in normally to see it.
- Contact the school if a PIN doesn't work.

## School fees

**Bursary → Fees & Wallet → School fees** shows a table of fees. For each fee:
- **Fee**, **Period**, **Amount Due**, **Payment Received** and **Balance**;
- a **Pay** button, or *Paid completely*.

Below it is the list of **Payments received**.

### Paying online
1. Choose **Pay ₦…** on a fee. Or use **Quick Portal Access → Pay Fees** on the home page.
2. eedu.ng shows a bank **Account number**, **Bank** and **Account name**, the exact **Total amount due**, and when it **expires** (a countdown).
3. Transfer **exactly** that amount from your bank app before it expires.
4. Choose **I have made this payment**. When the payment is confirmed you'll see **Payment Successful!**. It's recorded against the fee straight away.

Tips:
- Pay the exact amount shown. Anything above the balance goes into the student's wallet.
- If the account expires before you pay, choose **Pay** again for a new one.
- You can also pay at the school's bursary. Ask for a receipt: the bursar records it on the student's account.

### Wallet statement
**Bursary → Fees & Wallet → Wallet statement** lists every wallet movement: top-ups at the bursary, canteen purchases, book purchases, refunds, and fee overpayments. It shows the balance after each.

The wallet pays for canteen food and bookstore books with the student's ID card. Top it up at the bursary (cash or bank transfer).

### My Books
**Bursary → Fees & Wallet → My Books** (when the school has a bookstore):
- the class **book list**, *Required* and *Recommended*, with prices;
- the **book pack**, if the school sells one through fees: how much is paid, and which books have been collected. "Paid. Collect your books from the bursary" means you can pick them up;
- **What I've bought:** books bought at the bookstore, with receipts.

## Lessons and assignments

**Academics → My Learning → My Lessons**
- New lessons are marked. Filter by **Subject**.
- Open a lesson to read the note, watch its **YouTube videos**, and open its **Files** (they open in Google Drive).
- Tick **I've finished this lesson** when you're done. Your teacher sees who has opened and finished each lesson.

**Academics → My Learning → My Assignments** groups work into:
- **To do**;
- **Handed in**;
- **Marked**;
- **Missed**.

To hand in:
1. Open the assignment, read the instructions, and check the due date.
2. Type your **Answer**, and/or attach **Files from your Google Drive** (connect your Google account the first time). This depends on what the teacher asked for.
3. Choose **Hand in**.
   - You can change it and **Hand in again** until the due date.
   - Late work is accepted only if the teacher allows it, and is marked *late*.
4. When the teacher returns it, you'll see **Your mark** and their feedback.

## CBT tests

**Academics → My Learning → My Tests** lists your tests:
- **Open now**;
- **Coming up**, with when they open;
- **Done**;
- **Closed**.

1. Choose **Start** on an open test and confirm. **Your timer starts now**, and it keeps running even if you leave the page.
2. Answer each question. *Choose all that apply* means more than one answer may be right. Use **Previous** and **Next**, or the numbered buttons, to move around. **Clear my answer** removes an answer.
3. Your answers **save automatically** as you go (watch the save status at the top). If your internet drops, keep going: answers are sent when it returns.
4. Choose **Submit test** when finished. When time runs out, the test is submitted for you with your saved answers.
5. Depending on your teacher's settings, you see your **score** straight away, after the test closes, or later from the teacher. You may also be able to **review** the correct answers after it closes.

Please stay on the test page. Leaving it is counted and your teacher can see how often you did.

If you started by mistake or had a problem, tell your teacher. Only a Dean or administrator can reset an attempt.

## My Conduct

**Academics → My Conduct** (when the school uses Pastoral Care) shows:
- **My house** and its position among the houses this term;
- **My points this term:** merits, demerits and the total;
- **Boarding:** your hostel and room, or *Day student*;
- **Shared with me:** commendations and notes your teachers chose to share with you.

## Messages

**Message Center → Compose** sends a message to a group of staff by role, for example the Bursar or the School Admin. **Inbox** shows messages sent to you. Keep messages polite and about school matters.
