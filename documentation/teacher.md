# Teacher and Form Teacher

Portals: `/eedu/teacher/` (Teacher) and `/eedu/form-teacher/` (Form Teacher)

A **Form Teacher** has everything a Teacher has, plus the class-teacher tasks marked **(Form Teacher)**. Read [Getting started](getting-started.md) first.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Your subjects come first.** You can only enter scores, write lessons and set tests for the subjects and classes assigned to you on your staff profile. If a screen says you have no subjects, ask a School Admin to open your staff profile, choose **Subjects**, and tick what you teach.
{% endhint %}

## Entering scores

**Academics → Exams & Results → Update Result**
1. Choose the exam (this term's exams are listed).
2. Choose the **Class**, **Stream** and **Subject**. Only your assigned subjects appear.
3. Each student has a box for each part of the marking scheme, for example *CA1 (20%)*, *CA2 (20%)* and *EXAM (60%)*. Type the scores and choose **Save** for that student.
4. The card shows the **Total**, **Grade** and **Remark**, and "Last updated by … at …".
5. To remove a student's scores for the subject, choose **clear** and confirm.
6. The side panel shows how many students are scored and how many are left.

Tips:
- Enter each part as the term goes, for example CA1 after the first test. You don't have to wait for the exam.
- If a student is missing from your class list, tell the School Admin. They manage the class list.
- Every change is recorded with your name and the time, and the Dean can see recent changes.

### (Form Teacher) Your class's results
Form Teachers also have, under **Exams & Results**:
- **Broadsheet:** your class's subject totals, total, average and position. **Print**, and **Publish** your class's results. See the [Dean manual](dean.md#publishing-results).
- **Print Result:** your class's published report cards.
  - If your school's plan doesn't include publishing or printing results, those buttons are replaced by a notice. You can still enter scores and view the broadsheet.
  - Click a student's comment to change it. It saves by itself.
  - Make comment changes after the final publish, because publishing again resets comments to the automatic ones.
- **Annual Broadsheet** and **Print Annual Result:** your class's annual results. Annual results are published by the Dean.
- **Download/Upload Excel:** score templates for your class. See the [Dean manual](dean.md#3-entering-scores-from-excel).
- **Track latest updates:** recent score entries.

## CBT (computer-based tests)

**Academics → E-learning → CBT** (when CBT is on)

### 1. Build your question bank
**CBT → Question Bank → New question**
- **Type:** *One answer*, *Several answers* or *True / False*.
- **Subject** and **Class** (or *Any class*).
- **Question** text.
- **Options:** 2 to 6. For *Several answers*, tick every correct option; otherwise choose the one correct option.
- **Marks** and an optional **Topic**.

Once students have answered a question in a test, only its wording and topic can change. **Duplicate** it to make a changed version.

### 2. Import many questions from Excel
**CBT → Import Questions**
1. Choose the **Subject** and **Download template**. It takes one question per row, with its options, the correct letter(s) and the marks, up to 500 per file.
2. Fill it in, upload it, and check the preview. Rows with problems show why.
3. Tick the rows to import and choose **Import N questions into** *subject*.

### 3. Create a test
**CBT → Tests → New test**

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Title | For example *First CA test* |
| Session, Term, Subject, Class, Stream | Who takes the test. Leave the stream as *Whole class* for everyone. |
| Instructions | Shown before the first question |
| Opens / Closes | The window in which students can start |
| Time allowed (minutes) | Each student's own timer |
| Questions | *Everyone gets all the questions*, or *Each student gets a random set* (then set *Questions per student*) |
| Show students their score | *As soon as they submit*, *After the test closes*, or *Never* (you share it) |
| Shuffle question order / Shuffle options | On by default. Makes copying harder. |
| Let students review correct answers after it closes | Optional |
| Gradebook (optional) | Link to an exam and choose which part of the marking scheme (for example CA1) the test counts towards |

Choose **Create test**, add questions from your bank, then **Publish**.
- **While scheduled or open:** you can move a published test **Back to draft** if nobody has started it, **Close test now**, or **Reopen** it later.
- **Once students have started:** only the title, instructions and times can change.

### 4. During and after the test
**CBT → Tests → Results**
- **Students** tab:
  - each student's status (*Not started*, *Writing now*, *Submitted*, *Time ran out*), score, percentage and minutes taken;
  - **Left page**: how often they switched away from the test. It's a hint, not proof of cheating;
  - the average, highest and lowest scores;
  - **Print** and **Download Excel**.
- **Questions** tab: questions most students got wrong come first, with how often each option was chosen. A low score with one popular wrong option often means the question or its answer key needs checking.
- **Gradebook** tab: send scores into the linked exam.
  - Each score is scaled (score ÷ test marks × the marking-scheme part's total).
  - Tick who to send (*New*, *Already sent*, or *Differs: check first*) and confirm.
  - Only that part of the score changes.
- **Reset** (Deans, School Admins and Portal Managers): lets a student take the test again, with a recorded reason.

How students take a test is described in the [Students and Parents manual](student-and-parent.md#cbt-tests).

## Remote Lessons

**Academics → E-learning → Remote Lessons** (when Remote Lessons are on)

### Lessons
**Lessons → New lesson**
1. Enter the **Title**, **Subject**, **Class** and **Stream** (or *Whole class*), and optionally the **Topic**, **Week** and **Term**.
2. Write the **Lesson note**. Links starting `https://` become clickable.
3. **YouTube videos:** upload your video to YouTube as *Public* or *Unlisted*, paste its link, and choose **Add video**. Private videos can't be added.
4. **Publishing:** *Not yet (draft)*, *Now*, or *From a date and time*.
5. **Create lesson.**
6. **Files:** once the lesson is saved, attach documents (PDF, Word, slides…).
   - **Connect Google Drive** the first time, then upload. Files go into **your own Google Drive** and are shared view-only with anyone who has the link. Students only see the link inside the lesson.
   - Tick *Stop students downloading, printing or copying new uploads* if needed.
   - Or **Paste a Google Drive link**: set the file in Drive to "Anyone with the link can view" first.
   - If you delete a file from Drive, or leave the school, students lose it. A school-owned Google account is safest for important material.
7. **Who has opened it** lists each student and when they opened the lesson.

The Lessons list shows each lesson's status (*Draft*, *Published*, *Scheduled*), how many students opened it, and its videos and files.

### Assignments
**Assignments → New assignment** (or **Add an assignment** from a lesson)
1. Enter the **Title**, **Subject**, **Class** and **Stream**, when it's **Due**, and what it's **Marked out of**.
2. Tick **Accept late work** if late submissions are allowed. They're marked *late*.
3. Write the **Instructions**, and choose how **Students answer**: *Typing an answer*, *Google Drive files*, or both.
4. Optionally link to an exam's marking-scheme part (**Gradebook**).
5. **Create assignment**, attach any files for students, and **Publish**.
   - **Close** stops submissions. **Reopen** allows them again.
   - After students have submitted, the class, subject and maximum score can't change.

### Marking
Open an assignment → **Mark**.
- Pick a student who has submitted. Read their answer and files, then enter a **Score** and **Feedback**.
- **Save and return** shows the mark to the student now. **Save, return later** keeps it hidden until you choose **Return all marked work**.
- **Gradebook** tab: send marks into the linked exam, scaled like CBT scores.

## Pastoral Care

**Academics → Pastoral Care** (when Pastoral Care is on)

### Recording behaviour
**Pastoral Care → Record Behaviour**
1. Find the student by name or passcode. Tick **Several students** to record the same entry for a group.
2. Choose the **Category**:
   - a *commendation*, which gives merit points;
   - a *concern*, which gives demerit points and has a severity of *minor*, *moderate* or *serious*.
3. Enter **When**, **Where** (optional), **What happened** and the **Action taken**:
   - *No action*;
   - *Warning*;
   - *Detention*;
   - *Parent contacted*;
   - *Referred to counsellor*: sends the entry to the counsellors;
   - *Other*.
4. Optionally set a **Follow up by** date, and tick **Show this entry to the student** to share it in the student's portal.
5. **Record.**

### Your entries
- You can **Edit** or **Withdraw** your own entries for **48 hours**. After that, ask a Dean or School Admin.
- Withdrawing needs a reason. Nothing is deleted.
- **Who sees your entries:** you, the student's form teacher, the Dean and School Admin, and the house master (commendations only). The student sees them only if you shared them.

### Welfare alerts
On any student's profile you may see a short red alert, such as "Allergy: see welfare note". Act on it. The note itself is only visible to the welfare team and the student's form teacher.

### (Form Teacher) Your class
For students in your class you can also:
- see **every** behaviour entry;
- **read and add welfare notes** (each time you open one, it's recorded in the access log);
- **Text the parent** about an entry. You see the exact message first, and it can be sent once per entry.

If you've been made a counsellor, welfare officer, house master or hostel master, see the [Pastoral Team manual](pastoral-team.md).

## Students' profiles

Open a student from anywhere their name is a link, for example your class list or pastoral lists. The profile shows:
- their QR ID card;
- **Message Student**;
- their pastoral summary, when Pastoral Care is on: house, boarding, points this term, welfare alerts, and the behaviour you're allowed to see.

## Lecturers

The **Lecturer** role is for tertiary institutions. Its portal (`/eedu/lecturer/`) has **not been built yet**, so lecturers can't use eedu.ng after signing in. Until it is, a Dean, HOD or School Admin can enter scores for them (Deans and School Admins can enter any subject). Contact eedu.ng support for progress on the lecturer portal. The tasks a lecturer will do are the same as a teacher's in this manual.
