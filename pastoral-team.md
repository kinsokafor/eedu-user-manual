# Pastoral Team

Pastoral Care (when switched on for your school) keeps each student's behaviour, welfare, counselling, house and boarding records together. It's built around one principle: **each person sees what they need, and nothing more.** Every opening of a sensitive record is logged.

This manual is for:
- **[Managers](#managers):** School Admins, Deans and Portal Managers;
- **[Counsellors](#counsellors);**
- **[Welfare officers](#welfare-officers);**
- **[House masters](#house-masters)** and **[hostel masters](#hostel-masters)**;
- **[Form teachers](#form-teachers)**, for their pastoral duties.

For recording behaviour as a teacher, see the [Teacher manual](teacher.md#pastoral-care). For gate incidents, see the [Security manual](security.md#recording-a-gate-incident).

---

## Who sees what

| Record | Who can record it | Who can read it |
|---|---|---|
| **Behaviour** (commendations and concerns) | Teachers, form teachers, HODs, Deans, School Admins; Security (gate incidents only) | Managers; the student's form teacher; whoever recorded it; the student's house master (commendations only); the student (only entries shared with them) |
| **Welfare notes** (health, allergy, family, wellbeing) | Managers, welfare officers, counsellors, the student's form teacher | The same people. Every other staff member sees only the short **alert** label, such as "Allergy: see welfare note". |
| **Counselling sessions** | Counsellors | **Only the school's counsellors.** Managers see that a session took place, the date and the counsellor, never what was said. |

**Rules for everyone:**
- **Nothing is deleted.** Entries are *withdrawn* with a reason and stay on record.
- **48-hour rule:** the author can edit or withdraw their own behaviour or welfare entry for 48 hours. After that, only a manager can change it, and every change keeps the earlier version.
- **Counselling notes can't be edited.** Add a follow-up session instead.
- **The access log** records every opening of a welfare note or counselling session, and every change, withdrawal, print, parent text, team change and bed change. Nobody can edit or delete it.

## Where to find things

**Academics → Pastoral Care**:

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Follow-ups due, students with repeated concerns, class summaries, top commendations, recent entries. Search any student to open their pastoral record. |
| **Record Behaviour** | Record a commendation or concern |
| **Counselling** | Counsellors' caseload; managers' safeguarding flags |
| **Welfare Alerts** | Every active alert you're allowed to see |
| **Houses** | House table, house points, and (managers) set-up and membership |
| **Boarding** | Hostels, rooms and beds |
| **Settings & Team** (managers) | Pastoral team, behaviour categories, SMS template, repeat-concern threshold |
| **Access Log** (managers) | Who opened or changed sensitive records |

A **student's pastoral record** has tabs for **Behaviour**, **Welfare** and **Counselling**, shown only if you can see them. It also has the student's house, boarding, points for the term, and alerts.

The same summary appears on the student's **profile page** (Management → Students → the student), with an alert banner at the top.

## Managers

School Admins, Deans and Portal Managers.

### Setting up
**Pastoral Care → Settings & Team**

1. **Pastoral team.** Choose a staff member and a duty, then **Add**:
   - **Counsellor:** the only people who can read counselling notes.
   - **Welfare officer:** reads and writes welfare notes for every student.
   - **House master:** choose the house. They see their house's commendations and award house points.
   - **Hostel master:** choose the hostel. They manage beds and room lists there.

   Set up houses and hostels first. Every team change is recorded in the access log.

   > Managers can add themselves as counsellors. Keep the counsellor list to trained counsellors; the access log shows any change.
2. **Behaviour categories.** eedu.ng starts you with common ones, which you can rename or add to:
   - commendations: excellent work, helpfulness, leadership, sport or arts;
   - concerns: lateness, uniform, disruption in class, bullying, fighting, exam malpractice, damage to property.

   For each category set its **Points**. Concerns always count as negative and commendations as positive. A category that has been used can't switch between the two. Untick **Active** to retire one.
3. **Parent SMS template.** It fills in `{student}`, `{class}`, `{category}`, `{date}`, `{action}` and `{school}`. **Use the default** restores the original.
4. **Flag a student after this many concerns in a term** (default 3). Students at or above this number appear under *Repeat concerns* on the Overview.

### Day to day
- **Overview:** check the follow-ups due and the repeat concerns. Open a student to see their full record.
- **Any behaviour entry:** open it to **Edit**, **Mark resolved** or **Reopen**, **Text the parent…**, or **Withdraw** (with a reason). Managers can do this at any time, and only managers can record a **suspension**.
- **Texting a parent:**
  - You see the exact message and the phone number first.
  - It can be sent **once per entry**, and only when bulk SMS is set up for the school.
  - If the student has no phone number on record, you're told.
- **Safeguarding flags:** when a counsellor flags a concern, your dashboard card and **Pastoral Care → Counselling** show it.
  - Speak with the counsellor, then choose **Acknowledge**.
  - You never see the counselling notes.
- **Counselling list:** as a manager you see when sessions took place and with which counsellor. That's enough to know support is happening, without the details.
- **Access Log:** filter by record type or person. Check it regularly.

### Houses
**Pastoral Care → Houses**
- **Set up houses:** a name, a colour, a motto (optional), and **Active**.
- **Members:** choose a class. Then either:
  - set each student's house;
  - tick students and **Put selected in…** a house;
  - use **Balance the class…**, which spreads students without a house evenly. You see the counts before and after, then **Assign them**.
- A student belongs to one house at a time.
- **House table:** each house's points this term, made of its members' behaviour points plus **awards**. **Print** it for assembly.
- **Award house points:** choose a house, the points (negative to deduct) and a reason, for example "Inter-house sports: 1st".

### Boarding
**Pastoral Care → Boarding**
1. **Add hostel:** a name and who it's for (*Boys*, *Girls* or *Mixed*).
2. Choose the hostel and **Add room**: a name and the number of **Beds**.
3. **Give a bed to:** filter by class, pick a student without a bed, pick a room with free beds, and choose **Allocate**.
   - Boys can't be placed in a girls' hostel, or girls in a boys' hostel.
   - A full room can't take another student.
4. Each room lists its students with their welfare alerts. For each student you can:
   - **Move to…** another room;
   - **Remove** them from boarding, which makes them a day student this term.
5. **Print room lists** for the hostel.

Students without a bed this term are day students. A room's beds can't be reduced below the number of students in it.

## Counsellors

**Pastoral Care → Counselling** is your caseload:
- **Safeguarding flags** you've raised that managers haven't acknowledged yet.
- **Referrals:** behaviour entries where a teacher chose *Referred to counsellor*, with who referred the student and why.
  - **Take this referral** marks it as yours.
  - **Record a session** opens the student's counselling tab linked to the referral.
- **Follow-ups due** in the next 7 days.
- **Sessions:** every session in the school, with the student, date, counsellor and reason.

### Recording a session
Open the student's pastoral record → **Counselling** → **Add session**:
1. **When**, and the **Reason**: *Academic*, *Behaviour*, *Family*, *Emotional*, *Health*, *Bullying*, *Career* or *Other*.
2. **Session notes**, and optional **Next steps** and **Follow up by** date.
3. Tick **Safeguarding concern** if managers need to know something needs attention. They are told *that* there is a concern, never *what* it is.
4. **Save session.**

Session notes can't be edited once saved. To add to a case, open the session and choose **Add follow-up**. Follow-ups are kept together as a thread.

Opening a session shows its notes and its thread, and is recorded in the access log. Only the counsellor who recorded a session can **Withdraw** it, with a reason.

You can also read and add **welfare notes** for any student.

## Welfare officers

Open a student's pastoral record → **Welfare** (or use the Welfare section of their profile) → **Add welfare note**:
1. Choose the **Type**: *Health*, *Family*, *Allergy*, *Wellbeing* or *Other*.
2. Write the **Note**, and optionally a **Review by** date.
3. Tick **Alert** for anything every teacher must know, such as a serious allergy, asthma or a medical condition. Write a short **alert text**, such as "Allergy: see welfare note".
   - Staff see only the alert text, never the note.
   - Keep the alert text free of private details.
4. **Save note.**

The welfare list shows each note's type, date, author and status, but not its text. Opening a note shows it and records the opening in the access log.

You can **Edit** or **Withdraw** your notes for 48 hours, and mark them **Resolved** when they no longer apply. Withdrawing a note also removes its alert.

**Welfare Alerts** lists every active alert in the school: a quick reference for staff briefings and trips.

## House masters

- On **Pastoral Care → Houses**, **Award house points** to your own house (with a reason). The house table shows every house's standing.
- You see the **commendations** of students in your house, in the Overview and on their pastoral record.

## Hostel masters

On **Pastoral Care → Boarding** you see **your hostel**. You can:
- give students beds, move them between rooms, or remove them from boarding;
- see each student's welfare alerts;
- print room lists.

Only managers can add hostels and rooms.

---

## Form teachers

For students in your class you can:
- see **every behaviour entry**, not just your own;
- **Text the parent** about an entry;
- read and add **welfare notes**. Opening one is logged.

Your dashboard card shows follow-ups due and recent concerns in your class.
